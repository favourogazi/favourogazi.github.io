About me

One builder. Several crafts.

I am Favour Ogazi — also known as Uchenna. I lead operations at Mighty, where I sit at the intersection of strategy and execution across payments, foreign exchange and treasury. My work is to take priorities that cut across the business and make them operationally executable.

Before Mighty, I helped build DNI Pay from a young security startup into a live digital payments platform, serving as Head of Business Management and Operations. Through FONO, the advisory firm I founded in 2023, I have worked with eighteen businesses and institutions on pricing, operating systems, growth and practical AI implementation.

Writing is how I process what I see and lead. Thousands of readers follow my essays on Substack, Medium and X. I believe documentation and clear communication are leadership skills, and I practise both. Outside work, I am a woman of faith who values friendship, good stories and a full life with room for ambition, curiosity and quiet.

Experience

What I do

Operating leadership at Mighty, and advisory work through FONO — grounded in execution, not theory.

Mighty Operations Lead · Dec 2025 – present

I joined Mighty as an operator supporting the founder when execution still depended heavily on the CEO’s office. Early work focused on turning priorities into organised execution: structuring internal communication, trackers and standard operating procedures, clarifying ownership, and bringing discipline to trading operations, partner relationships and FX workflows.

As Operations Lead, the role widened into cross-functional operating ownership — client and partner onboarding, compliance processes, agreements and commercials, reporting, product operations and workflow design. The standing question is simple: how do we take something commercially promising and make it operationally executable, from regulated onboarding through money movement, settlement and go-live?

The work then moved closer to revenue: foreign exchange, rates, liquidity, settlement, collections and payouts, including direct trading. Today the focus is operating leadership — company-wide cadence, onboarding and due diligence, partners through to go-live, and building the trade desk so more of the company can execute well through systems and people, not through any single person.

Operations · 2023 – 2025

Building DNI Pay from the ground up

DNI began as a security startup and relaunched as DNI Pay, a digital payments platform. As Head of Business Management and Operations, I redefined team structure and OKRs, wrote the twelve-month market entry plan, led the public launch, and built customer experience, KYC, HR and finance systems at go-live — alongside five new partnership agreements.

Outcome: More than 2,000 active users within 21 days of launch; over 95% of KYC completed within 24 hours; 90% of issues resolved within 24 hours.

Content & growth · via FONO · 2024 – 2025

Turning Lagos Liga into a content business

One of Africa’s largest private recreational football leagues had energy on the pitch and little of it online. Through FONO I took over five platforms at once, spotlighted every stakeholder, and repositioned the league as a content business that stays relevant between matches.
